Final-year project grading · interactive portfolio demo

A jury grades a final-year project against one rubric. Assay does the arithmetic and shows students why.

Reviewers click the level description that matches the work. Assay weights each criterion, averages the reviewers into a team grade, tracks due dates and defenses, and releases results to students one assessment at a time.

Follow one grade in five minutes

  1. 1As Jane, jury member: finish the final report draft for Team Beta and submit it.
  2. 2Switch to Rania, coordinator: watch Beta's cell turn from "2 of 3" into a grade, then release it.
  3. 3Switch to a student: read the grade criterion by criterion, with the jury's comments.

Switching role keeps your data, so your own submission follows you.

About your private copy

Your first click creates a fresh copy of the demo data, just for this browser. Nobody else sees what you grade or release. Every copy is deleted at midnight, Paris time, and you can start over at any point with "Reset my demo".

Good to know

Clearing your browser data or opening a private window starts a new copy. The demo stores a random session ID, not personal data.
