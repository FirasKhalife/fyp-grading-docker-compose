Assay: a calm, exact place to grade final-year projects

To assay is to test something against a standard and certify the result. That is the product's job: several reviewers, one shared rubric, a traceable grade. The brand borrows the hallmark, the small stamp that certifies a metal. Here it certifies a level.

01

Three names

AssayPreferred

"Test against a standard, then certify." Exactly what a jury does with a rubric. Short, unusual in edtech, easy to say in a demo, and it gives the brand a visual idea: the hallmark stamp that carries the level.

In use: "Grade it in Assay." "Assay · Final report · Team Beta".

Juried

Says plainly what happens: work judged by a panel. Reads well inside sentences ("Juried by 3 reviewers"), but it is an adjective, so it is weaker as the name of the place you open every morning.

Advisors are not jury, which makes the name slightly wrong for one of the four roles.

Viva

From viva voce, the oral defense. Warm and memorable, and it fits the June defense days well.

It names one checkpoint out of five, and many universities already use it for the PhD exam, so it can confuse.

05

The level mark

One mark everywhere a level or score appears: grading cards, grid cells, student results.
{{l.v}} {{l.name}} {{l.hex}} · score band {{l.band}}
{{l.v}} {{l.name}} {{l.hex}} · dark
Rules
  • Three cues, always together: filled bars (shape), the numeral, the name. Colour is a fourth cue on top. Nothing depends on telling hues apart.
  • One hue, more ink as the level rises. No red-to-green, so a 1 reads as "early", not "failed", and the scale is safe for colour-blind reviewers.
  • Not demonstrated (0) is an empty dashed tile: absent, not alarming. Empty bars are outlined, not just faded.
  • Scores use the same bands: 0–12.4 → 0, 12.5–37.4 → 1, 37.5–62.4 → 2, 62.5–87.4 → 3, 87.5–100 → 4. A score of 88.0 carries the Exemplary mark.
  • Contrast: text on every tile passes 4.5:1. A selected card also gets a 2 px ink border and a check, so the selection never relies on a fill a reviewer may not see.
